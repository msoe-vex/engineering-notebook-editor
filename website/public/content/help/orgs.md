# Organizations & access

Everything you write sits under an **organization**. Notebooks inherit org membership; finer ACL is available per notebook.

## Organizations

- Create an org from **Home**.
- **People** lists active members and pending invites; alumni (when shown) are read-only.
- **Teams** group people for restricted notebook grants.
- **Settings** covers org name, branding, and public-page options.
- Org **owners** and **admins** manage invites, teams, and most notebook settings.

## Invites

Admins send invites by email. Recipients must sign in with a matching (or verified secondary) email. Expired invites can be resent from People.

## Notebook access

In notebook **Setup** or **Share**:

- **Org members** — everyone in the org can open the notebook (default for many teams).
- **Restricted** — only listed people and teams (plus org admins/owners as configured) can open it.

“Public” in product copy can mean different things:

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Org public page | Marketing page at `/o/…` (about, events, etc.) |
| Public PDF link | Shareable compiled PDF URL from Compile (optional) |
| Org-open notebook | Any org member can edit/view — not the open internet |

## Trash, templates, and Hide from PDF

- Deleted entries go to **Trash** (restorable).
- **Templates** live in the explorer; filter by Templates or use **New → Blank template / from template**.
- **Hide from PDF** keeps an entry in the notebook but excludes it from compile (alongside templates and trash).

## ZIP backup

Use notebook overflow **Export ZIP** / **Import ZIP** for offline archives. Import is last-write-wins — see [Backup & ZIP](/help/data).
